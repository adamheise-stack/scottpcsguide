## Where to start

- **Child care for PCS (Air and Space Force families):** funded by the Air & Space Force Aid Society. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says service members within 60 days of arriving may receive up to $200 per child (up to age 12), with a maximum of $1,000 per family. Call the M&FRC (618-256-8668) or see [afas.org](https://afas.org/). *Checked October 18, 2026.*
- **On-base child development and youth programs:** see [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS: Child and Youth Services](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base).
- **Request care through** [MilitaryChildCare.com](https://militarychildcare.com/), the DoD portal for military child care programs.
