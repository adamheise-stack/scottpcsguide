Congratulations on your orders! Scott sits in the Metro East, the Illinois side of the St. Louis area, surrounded by towns like Shiloh, O'Fallon, Belleville, Mascoutah, and Fairview Heights. The official information you need is real but spread across several websites. This guide gathers it into one timeline and links each official page so you can confirm the details.

*Every fact below was checked against the linked source on October 18, 2026. Phone numbers and policies change, so please confirm before you rely on them.*

## 60+ days out: get connected

- **Connect with your sponsor.** If you have not been assigned one within 60 days of departure, [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/base-essentials/check-in-procedures) advises contacting your gaining unit or the M&FRC.
- **Join the M&FRC's virtual Pre-Arrival Q&A.** The M&FRC holds it monthly for inbound members and families, covering Scott and the greater St. Louis area. Find it on the [workshop calendar](https://www.scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Workshops).
- **Build your moving timeline** with [Military OneSource Plan My Move](https://planmymove.militaryonesource.mil/), and set up your household goods shipment through [Move.mil](https://www.move.mil/).
- **Childcare:** waitlists move slowly in most places, so request care through [MilitaryChildCare.com](https://militarychildcare.com/) as early as you can.

## 30 days out: housing and schools

- **Start with the Military Housing Referral Office.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says prospective residents should begin their application there, whether or not they end up in the privatized Scott Family Housing that Hunt Companies manages.
- **Choose a home with schools in mind.** On base, children attend [Mascoutah CUSD 19](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/School-Liaison-Office/). Off base, your address determines your district, and many local districts are split into separate preK-8 and high school districts. Our [Schools page](/schools/) explains more.
- **Know your budget.** Check your 2026 rate in our [Scott AFB BAH guide](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/), which also compares it to average local rents.

## 7 days out: lodging and paperwork

- **Lodging:** request temporary lodging as soon as you know your arrival date. Per the [Newcomers page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/), you'll need a copy of your PCS orders. Incoming PCS personnel may stay up to 30 days if space is available. If base lodging is full, lodging issues a non-availability letter and reserves off-base lodging. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS notes that the lodging office is the base's 24-hour arrival point.
- **Pets:** [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base) lists a limited number of pet-friendly lodging units for an additional $10 per night, and you'll need a current rabies certificate at check-in.
- **Hand-carry your documents:** all PCS orders and amendments, receipts, and the records your family will need for schools and medical appointments. The [Finance in-processing page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Units/Finance/PCS-In-processing/) lists orders, amendments, and receipts for the finance brief.
- **Car seats:** Illinois law requires children through age 8 to ride in an appropriate car seat in the rear seat, and children under 2 to ride rear-facing unless they are at least 40 pounds or 40 inches tall ([MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base)).

## Your first week

1. **Follow your orders and your sponsor's instructions** for reporting. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says you'll get an installation in-processing checklist on your first duty day.
2. **Attend Newcomers Orientation.** It's mandatory for newly arrived active duty members and new DoD civilians, and spouses are encouraged to come. The M&FRC calendar says it includes an overview of base services and a Medical Right Start session. Register on the [M&FRC calendar](https://www.scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Workshops).
3. **Finish finance in-processing within 5 duty days of arrival.** The [Finance page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Units/Finance/PCS-In-processing/) describes this as a mandatory, appointment-based brief that reimburses travel and updates your pay entitlements for the new duty station.
4. **Update TRICARE** for your new location at [tricare.mil](https://www.tricare.mil/).
5. **Ask about child care for PCS.** Air and Space Force families within 60 days of arrival may get up to $200 per child (up to age 12), with a maximum of $1,000 per family, through the Air & Space Force Aid Society ([MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base); [afas.org](https://afas.org/)).
6. **Drive carefully.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS notes that cell phone use while driving is prohibited on base and texting while driving is illegal throughout Illinois.

## Getting around without a car (yet)

- **MetroLink:** the Red Line runs between St. Louis Lambert Airport and the [Shiloh-Scott station](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink-station/shiloh-scott-station/). [USTRANSCOM](https://www.ustranscom.mil/cmd/base-information.cfm) estimates the trip at about 1 hour 15 minutes, and asking your sponsor to meet you is a good idea.
- **SCCTD Flyer:** an on-demand shared ride within a [Scott AFB service zone](https://scctd.org/scott-air-force-base-safb-flyer-service-zone/) that connects to MetroLink and to places like the BX, commissary, clinic, pharmacy, and base housing. Checked October 18, 2026, fares were $3 per one-way trip within the base zone, with no cash accepted.
- **MidAmerica St. Louis Airport:** SCCTD runs a [shuttle](https://scctd.org/services/midamerica-airport-shuttle/) between the Shiloh-Scott station and the airport terminal, about a 20-minute trip.

## Winter arrivals

From late fall through winter, MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says you can check base conditions, reporting delays, and closures on the Scott AFB information line at 618-256-7232 or at [scott.af.mil](https://www.scott.af.mil/).
