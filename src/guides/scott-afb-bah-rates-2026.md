A few honest takeaways from comparing the two tables:

- **E-5 with dependents ($1,542)** matches the listed average for a 3-bedroom house ($1,542) exactly, and it's a bit above the averages for 3-bedroom apartments and townhouses.
- **E-1 to E-4 with dependents ($1,536)** gets nearly the same rate as an E-5 with dependents, so a 3-bedroom townhouse or apartment is within average range.
- **O-4 and above with dependents** clears the 4-bedroom house average ($2,205).
- **Rent isn't the whole bill.** BAH is calculated from median rent *plus* utilities (electricity, heat, water/sewer), per the [DoD release](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4357076/department-of-war-releases-2026-basic-allowance-for-housing-rates/). Ask landlords which utilities are included. If you're thinking about buying, factor in local property taxes, which BAH isn't based on ([Air Force benefits fact sheet](https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing-(BAH)?serv=26)).

## The fine print (what the rate tables skip)

- **Your duty ZIP decides the rate, not where you live.** DTMO says BAH depends on your grade, dependency status, and *permanent duty station* ZIP code ([DTMO](https://www.travel.dod.mil/allowances/basic-allowance-for-housing/bah-rate-lookup/)). If you live in Missouri or a farther Illinois town, you still receive the Scott rate.
- **2026 rates rose 4.2% on average nationwide.** DoD released them December 11, 2025, effective January 1, 2026. It surveys 299 military housing areas ([DoD release](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4357076/department-of-war-releases-2026-basic-allowance-for-housing-rates/)). Individual areas can rise or fall by different amounts.
- **There's a built-in out-of-pocket share.** The 2026 rates continue a member cost share of 5% of the national average housing cost, which works out to $93 to $212 per month depending on grade and dependency status (DoD release).
- **Rate protection.** If rates drop next year, you keep your current rate as long as your duty location, grade, and dependency status stay the same and your eligibility is uninterrupted (DoD release).
- **"With dependents" is one rate,** not a per-person amount ([MilitaryPayDates](https://militarypaydates.com/bah-rates/il/scott-afb-il/)).
- **Living on base?** Ask the Military Housing Referral Office (618-256-4900) how rent works for privatized Scott Family Housing before you compare it with off-base options. Start with our [Housing page](/housing/).
- **New rates every January.** The 2026 rates were announced December 11, 2025. We'll update this page when DoD publishes the 2027 rates.
