Most of what's online about Scott AFB lodging is spread over three or four official pages that don't quite agree with each other, plus hotel listings that weren't written with PCS families in mind. We pulled it together and noted where the official pages differ, so you know which questions to ask when you call. Everything below is from public sources, checked on October 23, 2026.

## Book early: how Scott Inn reservations work

- **Who can stay:** people on TDY or PCSing to Scott, active duty and retirees traveling in the area, and people visiting military members, if space is available ([Scott Inn](https://375fss.com/scott-inn/)).
- **Who gets priority:** PCS families. Reservations are first-come, first-served without regard to rank, and the Scott Inn says its family units "fill up fast" ([Scott Inn](https://375fss.com/scott-inn/), [Scott AFB Newcomers](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/)).
- **When to call:** as soon as you know your arrival date. Your sponsor can make the request for you ([Scott AFB Newcomers](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/)).
- **Who to call:** the Scott Inn at **618-256-1844** (DSN 576-1844), which [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/temporary-housing) lists as open 24/7. The Newcomers page lists **618-256-2045** for PCS reservation requests. Try either one.
- **What to have ready:** a copy of your PCS orders, due before or at check-in. House hunters need their permissive TDY orders or leave authorization plus the PCS, retirement, or separation orders ([Scott AFB Newcomers](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/)).
- **Don't count on it until it's confirmed:** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says accepted reservation requests "cannot be confirmed until a space is available." Ask lodging how you'll know when your room is confirmed.

## How long you can stay

The two official sources don't say the same thing:

- The [Scott AFB Newcomers page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/) says **30 days** for incoming PCS personnel, **7 days** for outgoing PCS personnel, and **10 days** for house hunters, all if space is available.
- [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/temporary-housing) says both inbound and outbound personnel may reserve **up to 30 days**.

Plan on the shorter limits and ask about an extension if you need one. Keep in mind that TLE usually pays for fewer days than lodging lets you stay (see below), so a long TLF stay can start coming out of your pocket.

## Bringing pets

- MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says there are **13 pet-friendly TLFs**. Ask for one when you make the reservation, not at check-in.
- They cost **$10 more a night**. Bring a **current rabies certificate** and your pet's vaccination records ([Scott Inn](https://375fss.com/scott-inn/)).
- If no pet unit is open, you'll need a kennel or a pet-friendly hotel. Several O'Fallon hotels take pets (listed below), and our [Local Services page](/local-services/) has more on getting settled.

## If base lodging is full: non-availability

You have to try military lodging first. If there's no room, lodging issues a non-availability letter and, according to the [Newcomers page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/), reserves lodging off base for you.

This step matters for your wallet. DTMO says you must use available government quarters, and to be paid TLE for a commercial hotel you need the **non-availability confirmation number** from the lodging reservation process. Without it, payment can be limited to the government lodging cost or the locality lodging rate, whichever is lower ([DTMO: Temporary Lodging Expense](https://www.travel.dod.mil/DesktopModules/ArticleCS/Print.aspx?Article=3174593&ModuleId=31462&PortalId=119)). Get the number in writing before you book a hotel.

## TLE: what the military pays back

Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) partly repays lodging and meals while you're in temporary lodging during a PCS within the continental U.S. Here's what [DTMO](https://www.travel.dod.mil/DesktopModules/ArticleCS/Print.aspx?Article=3174593&ModuleId=31462&PortalId=119) says, as published December 2, 2024:

- **Up to 21 days** in total for a move to a duty station in the continental U.S. (it was 14 days before November 27, 2024). You can split the days between your old and new duty stations ([PDTATAC memo, November 2024](https://media.defense.gov/2024/Dec/03/2003597426/-1/-1/0/UTD_FOR_MAP_66-24(R),_TEMPORARY_LODGING_EXPENSE_(TLE)_PERIOD_INCREASE.PDF)).
- **No more than $290 a day**, and none on days you're paid PCS travel per diem.
- **Itemized lodging receipts are required.** Keep every one.
- **Staying with friends or family?** You get only the meal portion, with no lodging reimbursement.
- **How it's figured:** the local per diem rate times a percentage based on who's staying with you. It's 65% for one person, 100% for two, plus 35% for each additional dependent age 12 or older and 25% for each one under 12.

### The Scott numbers for FY2027

For October 1, 2026, through September 30, 2027, GSA's per diem rate for ZIP 62225 (the St. Clair County locality, "East St. Louis / O'Fallon / Fairview Heights") is **$156 a night for lodging** and **$86 a day for meals and incidentals** ([GSA FY2027 per diem, ZIP 62225](https://www.gsa.gov/travel/plan-book/per-diem-rates/per-diem-rates-results?action=perdiems_report&city=&fiscal_year=2027&zip=62225)). For FY2026 the lodging rate was $150.

Here's an **illustration only** of DTMO's four steps for a family of four (the member, a spouse, and two kids under 12) staying in a $155 TLF:

1. Percentage: 100% + 25% + 25% = 150%. The lodging ceiling is $156 × 1.5 = $234, and meals are $86 × 1.5 = $129.
2. The $155 TLF costs less than the $234 ceiling, so $155 counts.
3. $155 + $129 = $284.
4. $284 is under the $290 cap, so that's about **$284 a day**.

A family of five with three kids under 12 works out to more than $290 (175% gives a $273 ceiling and $150.50 for meals), so it would be capped at **$290**. Your finance office makes the actual calculation, so treat these numbers as a planning estimate and ask finance during in-processing. ## Hotels near Scott AFB if you need them

If you end up off base, most families look at O'Fallon, Shiloh, and Fairview Heights, close to I-64. We haven't stayed at these and aren't recommending one over another. These are just places whose own pages describe extended-stay or pet policies, as of October 23, 2026:

- [Candlewood Suites O'Fallon, IL - St. Louis Area](https://www.ihg.com/candlewood/hotels/us/en/o-fallon/stlof/hoteldetail): all-suite extended stay with full kitchens. Pets are $25 a night, up to $75 for stays of up to 6 nights and $150 for 7 or more, with a 2-pet maximum.
- [Welcome Suites O'Fallon](https://welcomesuites.com/welcome-suites-ofallon/): extended stay with in-room kitchens and guest laundry. Pets are $75 at check-in plus $10 per pet per night, with a 50-pound limit and 2 pets per room.

Before you book, ask the hotel for its military rate, make sure the room is under the lodging ceiling you'll be reimbursed for, and get an itemized receipt. If you're still choosing a town, our [best places to live guide](/guides/best-places-to-live-near-scott-afb/) compares them side by side.

## A quick lodging checklist

- [ ] Call the Scott Inn (618-256-1844) as soon as you have an arrival date, or ask your sponsor to.
- [ ] Ask for a TLF, and ask for a pet-friendly unit if you need one.
- [ ] Ask how long you can stay and how you'll know your reservation is confirmed.
- [ ] Have a copy of your PCS orders ready to give lodging (and permissive TDY or leave paperwork if you're house hunting).
- [ ] If lodging is full, get the non-availability confirmation number before you book a hotel.
- [ ] Keep every itemized lodging receipt for your TLE claim.
- [ ] Count your TLE days. You get 21 in total for the whole move, not 21 at each end.
- [ ] Line up permanent housing. The [Military Housing Referral Office](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/housing) (618-256-4900) helps with on- and off-base options, and our [Housing page](/housing/) has the basics.
- [ ] Use our [Moving Checklist](/moving-checklist/) for everything else.
