So we pulled public numbers for the towns families most often choose and put them side by side: distance, rent, home values, which school districts actually serve each town, and whether you can catch MetroLink there. Use this to make a short list, then go drive the neighborhoods.

**How to read it:** "Median gross rent" covers every rental in town, from one-bedroom apartments to houses, and it includes utilities. A family-size house will usually cost more than the median. For a better sense of what bigger homes cost, see the average Scott-area rents by bedroom count in our [2026 BAH guide](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/).

## Closest to the base: Shiloh and Mascoutah

**Shiloh** is the closest town on the O'Fallon side, and it has the Shiloh-Scott MetroLink station, the Illinois end of the Red Line ([Metro Transit](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink/)). It's also the trickiest town for schools. When we checked addresses across the village, they fell into four different elementary districts (Shiloh 85, Whiteside 115, Mascoutah CUSD 19, and Central 104) and two high school districts (O'Fallon Township 203 and Belleville Township 201). Two houses on neighboring streets can feed completely different schools, so check every listing.

**Mascoutah** is just as close and the simplest for schools: every point we checked in the city came back as Mascoutah CUSD 19, one preK-12 district, and it's the same district that serves children living in on-base housing. If you might move on base later, or off base after starting there, your kids can stay in the same district. Its median rent ($1,370) is the highest in the table, while its median home value ($224,900) is in the middle.

## The big suburb: O'Fallon

**O'Fallon** is the largest town close to the base (32,656 people in the Census Bureau's 2025 estimate) and has the highest median home value of the close-in towns, at $295,700. Most addresses are in O'Fallon CCSD 90 for elementary and middle school, with some in Central 104. Every point we checked went on to O'Fallon Township High School District 203. If you buy here, look at the property tax line on each listing (more on that below). Census puts the median monthly owner cost with a mortgage at $2,105, the highest of any town in the table.

## Most budget room: Belleville, Swansea, and Fairview Heights

**Belleville** is the St. Clair County seat and has the lowest median rent ($1,019) and home value ($141,300) of the towns we compared. It also has three MetroLink stations: Belleville, College, and Memorial Hospital. Most of the city is in Belleville District 118 and Belleville Township High School District 201. Some edges fall into other districts (we found Belle Valley 119, Harmony Emge 175, and Freeburg 70/77), so confirm before you sign.

**Swansea** sits between Belleville and O'Fallon. Its median home value ($223,600) is close to Mascoutah's, and its median rent is $1,137. It has its own MetroLink station. Elementary districts vary by street (Wolf Branch 113, Belleville 118, or High Mount 116), and high schoolers go to Belleville 201.

**Fairview Heights** is about 9 miles out. It has a MetroLink station where both the Red Line and the Blue Line stop, a median rent of $1,174, and a median home value of $177,700. Most addresses are in Grant 110 or Pontiac-William Holliday 105 and then Belleville 201, but a few are in O'Fallon 203.

## Small-town and farther-out options

**Lebanon** is about 4.6 miles from the base and is home to [McKendree University](https://www.mckendree.edu/). The points we checked came back as Lebanon CUSD 9, one preK-12 district. Lebanon has fewer than 5,000 residents, so Census QuickFacts doesn't publish rent or value figures for it. Check current listings to get a feel for prices.

**Edwardsville and Glen Carbon** are in Madison County, about 17 to 19 miles north in a straight line. They're farther than everything else here, but both are almost entirely in Edwardsville CUSD 7, and Edwardsville is home to [Southern Illinois University Edwardsville](https://www.siue.edu/). Median home values are close to O'Fallon's ($299,800 and $282,000). Before you commit, drive the route at the time you'd actually be commuting.

## Your BAH vs. the local numbers

The 2026 Scott AFB BAH is $1,536 a month for E-1 to E-4 with dependents, $1,542 for E-5, and $2,208 for O-3 (effective January 1, 2026; full table in our [BAH guide](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/)). Every town's median gross rent is below even the lowest with-dependents rate. That's encouraging, but the median includes small apartments, so it's a floor, not a promise.

If you're thinking about buying, Census also reports the **median monthly owner cost with a mortgage**, which includes the mortgage, property taxes, insurance, and utilities. In 2020-2024 it was $2,105 in O'Fallon, $2,096 in Glen Carbon, $2,040 in Edwardsville, $1,946 in Shiloh, $1,877 in Mascoutah, $1,872 in Swansea, $1,454 in Fairview Heights, and $1,445 in Belleville ([Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/ofalloncityillinois,shilohvillageillinois,mascoutahcityillinois,bellevillecityillinois,swanseavillageillinois,fairviewheightscityillinois/PST045224)). These numbers are for current owners, many of whom bought years ago, so a new purchase at today's prices and rates will usually cost more.

## Don't skip the property tax math

This is the part that surprises people coming from other states. The [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/state/property-taxes-by-state-county-2024/) ranked Illinois second-highest in the U.S. for effective property tax rate on owner-occupied homes in 2022, at 1.95% of home value. [Illinois Policy](https://www.illinoispolicy.org/illinois-property-tax-rate-tops-u-s-but-pain-worse-in-some-areas/) (September 2025) puts St. Clair County at 2.09% for 2023.

As a rough example, a 2% effective rate on a $295,700 home (O'Fallon's median) works out to about $5,900 a year, or about $490 a month, on top of the mortgage. That's only an illustration. Real bills depend on the taxing districts, the assessment, and exemptions such as Illinois' homestead exemptions, so look up the actual tax bill for any house you're considering. BAH isn't set to cover property taxes, so this money comes out of the same check.

## Living in Missouri instead

Some families live on the Missouri side. Your BAH stays the same because it's based on your duty station, not your home ([Air Force Benefits](https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing-(BAH)?serv=26)). The trade-offs are a longer commute and different state taxes. We'll compare the two states in a separate guide. For now, our [Housing page](/housing/) covers the basics.

## How to choose (a quick checklist)

- [ ] **Pick your school district first, then the house.** Use the [St. Clair County Regional Office of Education](https://sccroe50.org/public-schools/) list, or type the exact address into the [Census Geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/address?form) to see which districts serve it. Then confirm with the district. Our [Schools page](/schools/) explains the elementary/high school split.
- [ ] **Do the drive at your real commute time.** Straight-line miles don't show traffic or which way you'll come in.
- [ ] **Want to skip some driving?** If MetroLink matters to you, start with Shiloh, Swansea, Belleville, or Fairview Heights. See [Local Services](/local-services/) for transit.
- [ ] **Budget from your BAH and include utilities.** For buyers, add property taxes and insurance too.
- [ ] **Check safety with real data, not rumors.** We don't rank towns as "good" or "bad." Look up official numbers in the [FBI Crime Data Explorer](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/), and visit a neighborhood in the evening before you sign.
- [ ] **Talk to the Military Housing Referral Office** (618-256-4900) before you sign a lease. They help with both on- and off-base housing ([MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/housing)).
- [ ] **Need time to look?** Base lodging allows house hunters up to 10 days and incoming PCS families up to 30 days, if space is available ([Scott AFB Newcomers](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/)). Our [first-week guide](/guides/scott-afb-pcs-first-week-guide/) lays out the timeline.
