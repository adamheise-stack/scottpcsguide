## Who runs this site

Scott PCS Guide is run by a local military family who's been through the move. We remember how many tabs it took to figure out housing, schools, and that first week, so we're putting the answers in one friendly place. It's an independent project. It is **not** an official Air Force, Department of Defense, or Scott AFB website, and nobody here speaks for the base.

## How we research

- We start from official sources (scott.af.mil, Military OneSource, DTMO, the base Military & Family Readiness Center) and link them on every page.
- Every guide shows a **Last verified** date. Rates, phone numbers, and policies change, so always confirm with the official source.
- We don't publish fake reviews, invented facts, or anything that could compromise operational security (no gate procedures, unit details, or schedules).

<h2 id="sponsorship">Sponsorship</h2>

The site is free because local businesses can sponsor it. Our rules:

- Every paid placement is visibly labeled **"Sponsored"** or **"Sponsor,"** and paid links carry `rel="sponsored"`.
- Paying never changes our free content, editorial guides, or what we say about a business.
- Sponsors must be legitimate local businesses. We don't accept payday or high-cost lenders, "military-approved" claims, or DoD/USAF insignia.
- Advertising does not constitute endorsement by the U.S. Air Force or Department of Defense of the products or services advertised.

Available placements: a homepage sponsor banner, featured listings on each topic page, an exclusive sponsor for each topic, and clearly labeled sponsored articles. Contact details for sponsorship are coming soon.
