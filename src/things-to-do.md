Most "things to do near Scott" lists are long and undated, with no hint of what costs money. Ours is shorter and sorted the way a neighbor would tell you: free first, close to home second, then day trips. Prices and seasons change, so every item links to the official page.

## Free in St. Louis (about 20 miles away)

[USTRANSCOM's base information page](https://www.ustranscom.mil/cmd/base-information.cfm) puts Scott AFB about 20 miles from downtown St. Louis.

- **Forest Park.** [Forest Park Forever](https://www.forestparkforever.org/visit) describes a free, 1,300-acre park with more than 30 miles of paths and trails. It's home to five major cultural institutions: the Saint Louis Art Museum, the Saint Louis Science Center, the Missouri History Museum, The Muny, and the Saint Louis Zoo.
- **Saint Louis Zoo.** [Admission is free](https://www.stlzoo.org/visit/), and the Zoo is open daily. It also sells an optional one-day Adventure Pass.
- **Saint Louis Art Museum.** The museum says [admission is free every day](https://www.slam.org/), and it loans strollers at no charge.
- **Gateway Arch museum and Old Courthouse.** The [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/fees.htm) says both are always free, with no ticket needed. The tram ride to the top costs extra (adult tickets start at $15 and vary by date), sells out on busy days, and all visitors go through airport-style security.

## The military pass that pays for itself

The [America the Beautiful Military Annual Pass](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm) is **free for current US military members and their dependents**. At the Gateway Arch, it takes $3 off each tram or documentary ticket, for up to four tickets per pass. It also covers entrance and standard day-use fees at federal recreation sites nationwide, which helps on road trips.

## Getting there on MetroLink

Metro's [MetroLink](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink/) light rail covers 46 miles and 38 stations in Missouri and Illinois. The Red Line runs from the Shiloh-Scott station to Lambert Airport, and both lines stop at every station between Fairview Heights and Forest Park-DeBaliviere. Buy tickets at station vending machines or in the Transit app, and validate any ticket that doesn't have a time stamp. See our [Local Services](/local-services/) page for buses and the SCCTD Flyer.

## Close to home in the Metro East

- **O'Fallon Family Sports Park.** [O'Fallon Parks & Recreation](https://ofallonparksandrec.com/parks/ofallon-family-sports-park/) lists playgrounds, sports fields, fishing, free parking, a 2.17-mile trail, and a 6,000-square-foot splash pad. The splash pad is open Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend. Dogs aren't allowed in this park.
- **Rock Springs Rotary Park (O'Fallon).** [Trails](https://ofallonparksandrec.com/parks/rock-springs-rotary-park/) (a 1-mile outer loop), a playground, pavilions, and the city's fenced off-leash dog park. Kids must be at least 10 to go into the dog park.
- **MetroBikeLink trails.** The [St. Clair County Transit District](https://scctd.org/metrobikelink/) runs more than 40 miles of connected trails. The main MetroBikeLink trail runs 18 miles beside MetroLink, from near MidAmerica St. Louis Airport to the Fairview Heights Transit Center. Its extension from the Shiloh-Scott Transit Center opened to the public in June 2026. The Orchard Loop Trail has a trailhead with parking at Eckert's, outside Belleville.

## On base

The [375th Force Support Squadron](https://375fss.com/) runs base recreation and posts a calendar of family events and ticket offers. Its [Outdoor Recreation](https://375fss.com/outdoor-recreation/outdoor-recreation-facility-overview/) office rents camping gear, camping trailers, canoes and sports equipment, sells on-base fishing permits, and manages Scott Lake, the picnic pavilions, the swimming pool and FamCamp reservations. Renters must be at least 18 and carry a valid DoD ID.

## Day trip: Cahokia Mounds

[Cahokia Mounds State Historic Site](https://cahokiamounds.org/) in Collinsville is the largest pre-Columbian site north of Mexico and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1982. At its peak around 1100, the city covered more than 4,000 acres. The site says the grounds are open daily from dawn until dusk and that leashed dogs are welcome. Part of the museum's exhibit gallery is currently closed, so check the site before you go. For more day-trip ideas in the region, [Great Rivers & Routes](https://www.riversandroutes.com/) is the tourism bureau for Madison County and nearby counties.
