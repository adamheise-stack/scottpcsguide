## Before you leave

- [ ] Connect with your sponsor. If you have not been assigned one, MilitaryINSTALLATIONS advises contacting your gaining unit or the Scott AFB M&FRC (618-256-8668).
- [ ] Build a custom timeline with [Military OneSource Plan My Move](https://planmymove.militaryonesource.mil/).
- [ ] Schedule your household goods shipment through [Move.mil](https://www.move.mil/).
- [ ] Register for the M&FRC's virtual Pre-Arrival Q&A ([workshop calendar](https://www.scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Workshops)).
- [ ] Request temporary lodging as soon as you know your arrival date (see our [first-week guide](/guides/scott-afb-pcs-first-week-guide/)).
- [ ] Contact the Military Housing Referral Office and the School Liaison Office.
- [ ] Get on childcare waitlists at [MilitaryChildCare.com](https://militarychildcare.com/).
- [ ] Gather records: orders, IDs, school and medical records, pet vaccination records.

## After you arrive

- [ ] Follow your in-processing checklist and attend Newcomers Orientation.
- [ ] Update TRICARE enrollment at [tricare.mil](https://www.tricare.mil/).
- [ ] Ask about Air & Space Force Aid Society child care for PCS (see [Childcare](/childcare/)).

A printable, more detailed checklist is coming soon.
