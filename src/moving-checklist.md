Your orders, your gaining unit and the base offices always have the final word, and [Plan My Move](https://planmymove.militaryonesource.mil/) will tailor a list to your family. What follows is the Scott-specific version we wish we'd had: the local offices, phone numbers and Illinois rules, in the order you'll need them.

## As soon as you have orders

- [ ] Build a custom timeline with [Military OneSource Plan My Move](https://planmymove.militaryonesource.mil/). You can save, download and print it.
- [ ] Connect with your sponsor. The [M&FRC](https://scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Assistance) says all Air Force members should be assigned one, and to contact the M&FRC (618-256-8668) if you haven't heard from yours within 90 days of your report-no-later-than date.
- [ ] Schedule your household goods shipment through [Move.mil](https://www.move.mil/).
- [ ] Request childcare at [MilitaryChildCare.com](https://militarychildcare.com/). MCC says to request care as soon as you know your duty station and rough arrival date, even before orders. See [Childcare](/childcare/).
- [ ] Register for the M&FRC's monthly virtual Pre-Arrival Q&A, held on Zoom ([workshop calendar](https://www.scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Workshops)).

## 30 to 60 days out

- [ ] Request temporary lodging as soon as you know your arrival date. The [Newcomers page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/) says reservation requests go to 618-256-2045, members must try military lodging first, and you'll need a copy of your PCS orders. Incoming PCS families can stay up to 30 days if space is available. See our [Scott AFB lodging and TLF guide](/guides/scott-afb-lodging-tlf-guide/) for rates, pets, and TLE, and our [first-week guide](/guides/scott-afb-pcs-first-week-guide/) for the timeline.
- [ ] Contact the Military Housing Referral Office (618-256-4900) and the School Liaison Office (618-256-9595). See [Housing](/housing/) and [Schools](/schools/).
- [ ] Look up the school district for any address before you sign a lease.
- [ ] Set up mail. The M&FRC says the Scott AFB Postal Service Center gives newcomers a free post office box during the transition (618-256-2956).
- [ ] Gather records: orders, IDs, school and medical records, pet vaccination records, and certified birth certificates (Illinois schools ask for them at registration).

## Moving week

- [ ] Hand-carry orders and amendments, travel receipts and lodging receipts. You'll need them for finance in-processing.
- [ ] Keep school records, immunization records and the kids' physicals with you, not in the shipment.
- [ ] Check the weather. The M&FRC says that if you arrive in bad weather, the Scott AFB Facebook page, website, or Straight Talk Line (618-256-7232) has late-reporting and closure information.

## After you arrive

- [ ] **Finance in-processing within 5 duty days.** The [Scott AFB finance office](https://www.scott.af.mil/Units/Finance/PCS-In-processing/) says the in-person brief is mandatory and by appointment, and that it reimburses travel and corrects your pay entitlements to Scott.
- [ ] **Newcomers Orientation.** Required for active-duty members and new DoD civilians, and spouses are encouraged to attend. Register through the M&FRC.
- [ ] **Borrow what you're missing.** The M&FRC's free Loan Locker lends folding tables, cookware, dishes and small appliances (no linens).
- [ ] **Household goods problems?** The Personal Property Office (TMO) is at 618-256-1201.
- [ ] Update TRICARE enrollment at [tricare.mil](https://www.tricare.mil/).
- [ ] Apply for Air & Space Forces Aid Society child care for PCS within 60 days of arrival, if you're eligible (see [Childcare](/childcare/)).
- [ ] Set up utilities. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS points newcomers to InMyArea.com to find providers by ZIP code. See [Local Services](/local-services/).

## Driver's licenses and car registration in Illinois

- **Staying a resident of your home state?** Illinois law ([625 ILCS 5/6-102](https://www.ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/062500050K6-102.htm)) exempts a nonresident on active duty who has a valid home-state license, plus their spouse and dependent children with valid home-state licenses, from needing an Illinois license.
- **Becoming Illinois residents?** The [Secretary of State](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/drivers/faq.html) says new residents can drive on a valid out-of-state license for 90 days, must surrender it at a DMV and pass a vision screening and written exam (possibly a drive exam), and must change vehicle registration within 30 days. Express facilities don't handle out-of-state transfers.
- **Not sure which applies?** The base legal office (618-256-5017) offers confidential consultations on topics including taxation.
