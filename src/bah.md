Most of the BAH pages that rank for Scott AFB are just a rate table. The table matters, and [ours is here](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/), but the questions we hear from neighbors are more practical: what does it really cover, does it change if we live in Missouri, and what should we ask before we sign a lease? That's what this page is for.

## How BAH is calculated

- **Three inputs, one rate.** DTMO says BAH depends on your pay grade, your dependency status, and your *permanent duty station* ZIP code ([DTMO BAH lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/allowances/basic-allowance-for-housing/bah-rate-lookup/)).
- **It's based on rent plus utilities.** The Air Force benefits site says rates come from local rental market data, that utility costs are considered, and that BAH is *not* based on homeownership costs like mortgage payments and property taxes ([Air Force Benefits: BAH](https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing-(BAH)?serv=26)).
- **It isn't meant to cover every dollar.** DTMO says BAH is "not intended to cover all of a service member's housing costs," and your out-of-pocket can be higher or lower depending on the home you choose ([DTMO BAH overview](https://www.travel.dod.mil/allowances/basic-allowance-for-housing/)). Our [BAH guide](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/) explains the built-in member cost share for 2026.
- **"With dependents" is one rate.** It doesn't grow with family size (DTMO).

## The 2026 numbers for Scott

The 2026 rates took effect **January 1, 2026**, after DoD announced an average nationwide increase of 4.2% ([DoD release, Dec. 11, 2025](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4357076/department-of-war-releases-2026-basic-allowance-for-housing-rates/)). Rather than repeat the figures here, we keep a single, dated table in one place so it's never out of sync:

DTMO notes that rents generally change between 2% and 5% a year, and housing allowances typically follow. New rates are published each January, and we'll update the guide when the 2027 rates come out.

## Using your BAH on a Scott house hunt

A few neighbor-tested habits that make the number go further:

1. **Budget for rent *and* utilities.** Since BAH assumes utilities, ask every landlord which ones are included. Electric, gas, water, trash and internet can vary a lot by town.
2. **Decide on your trade-off early.** The Air Force benefits site points out that you can spend more to live close to base or rent a larger home farther out. The base's [Military & Family Readiness Center](https://scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Assistance) names Shiloh, O'Fallon, Belleville, Mascoutah, Fairview Heights and the greater St. Louis area as the surrounding communities, so you have several towns to compare.
3. **Check the school district before you sign.** In the Scott area, the school your child attends follows your address. See our [Schools](/schools/) page.
4. **Compare on-base housing too.** Privatized Scott Family Housing is managed by Hunt Companies. Start with the Military Housing Referral Office (618-256-4900), per [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/housing). See our [Housing](/housing/) page.

## The fine print worth knowing

- **Rate protection.** If rates drop, DTMO says you keep the higher of the January 1 rate or what you received on December 31, as long as your status doesn't change. A PCS, a reduction in grade, or a change in dependency status ends that protection.
- **Partial BAH.** A member without dependents who lives in government quarters receives partial BAH (Air Force Benefits).
- **Living apart during the move?** The Air Force offers Secretarial Housing Allowance Waivers in limited PCS situations where a member and dependents live separately. The Air Force benefits page lists the eligible situations; ask your finance office.
- **Finance in-processing.** The Scott AFB finance office says PCS finance in-processing must be done within 5 duty days of arrival, and that it corrects your pay entitlements to your new duty station ([Scott AFB Finance](https://www.scott.af.mil/Units/Finance/PCS-In-processing/)).
