## Quick facts

- BAH is set by your **permanent duty station ZIP code**, pay grade, and dependency status ([DTMO BAH lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/allowances/basic-allowance-for-housing/bah-rate-lookup/)).
- 2026 rates took effect **January 1, 2026**, with an average nationwide increase of 4.2% ([DoD release, Dec. 11, 2025](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4357076/department-of-war-releases-2026-basic-allowance-for-housing-rates/)).
- For the full Scott AFB rate table and what it buys locally, read the guide below.

## Official calculator

Always confirm your exact rate in the [official DTMO BAH calculator](https://www.travel.dod.mil/allowances/basic-allowance-for-housing/bah-rate-lookup/) or with your finance office.
