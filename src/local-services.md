Once the boxes are in the house, the questions get smaller but more frequent: how do I get downtown, who turns on the gas, who do I call about a will? Here's the short list, with the official page for each.

## Getting around

- **MetroLink.** [Metro](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink/) runs 46 miles of light rail across 38 stations in Missouri and Illinois, seven days a week from before 5 a.m. to about 1 a.m. The Red Line runs between Lambert Airport Terminal 1 and the [Shiloh-Scott station](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink-station/shiloh-scott-station/). Buy tickets at station vending machines or in the Transit app. For trip help, call Metro Transit Information at 618-271-2345.
- **SCCTD Flyer.** An on-demand shared ride that the St. Clair County Transit District runs in a [Scott AFB service zone](https://scctd.org/scott-air-force-base-safb-flyer-service-zone/). The zone connects MetroLink, the #12 O'Fallon-Fairview Heights MetroBus and the MidAmerica Airport shuttle with Shiloh Housing, Patriots Landing, the pharmacy, the clinic, the BX and the commissary. As of October 18, 2026, fares were $3 per one-way trip within the base zone ($1 with a Senior or Benefit Access Pass), with no cash fares. SCCTD sells fares in its VIA SCCTD Flyer app and at MetroLink ticket machines in Illinois. The Scott AFB Newcomers page lists 618-800-6884 for scheduling a ride.
- **MidAmerica St. Louis Airport.** SCCTD runs a [shuttle](https://scctd.org/services/midamerica-airport-shuttle/) between the Shiloh-Scott MetroLink station and the airport terminal. SCCTD also lists a 5.2-mile [MetroLink extension to MidAmerica](https://scctd.org/midamerica-st-louis-airport-metrolink-extension-2/) as opening in 2026, so check its page for current service.
- **By bike.** The 18-mile MetroBikeLink trail runs beside MetroLink. See [Things to Do](/things-to-do/).

- **Visitor passes and base access:** see our [visitor center and gate info](/visitor-center/) page, which links only to official sources.

## Utilities

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS points newcomers to [InMyArea.com](https://www.inmyarea.com/) to find utility providers by ZIP code. Two neighbor tips:

- **If your electric and gas provider is Ameren Illinois,** its [start-service FAQ](https://www.ameren.com/Illinois/Account/Start-Stop-Move/Start-FAQs) says you'll need the account holder's name, the service address, a Social Security number and a start date. Electricity must be on before gas can be turned on, and some new customers may be asked for a deposit.
- **Water and trash** depend on your town or landlord, so ask during your lease signing.

## Driver's licenses

Illinois law exempts an active-duty nonresident with a valid home-state license, plus their spouse and dependent children with valid home-state licenses, from needing an Illinois license. If you become Illinois residents, the Secretary of State gives you 90 days on your old license and 30 days to change your vehicle registration. Details are on our [Moving Checklist](/moving-checklist/).
