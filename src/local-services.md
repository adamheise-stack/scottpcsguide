## Getting around

- **MetroLink:** the Red Line runs between Lambert Airport and the [Shiloh-Scott station](https://www.metrostlouis.org/metrolink-station/shiloh-scott-station/).
- **SCCTD Flyer:** an on-demand shared ride that the St. Clair County Transit District runs in a [Scott AFB service zone](https://scctd.org/scott-air-force-base-safb-flyer-service-zone/). Checked October 18, 2026, fares were $3 per one-way trip within the base zone.
- **MidAmerica St. Louis Airport:** SCCTD runs a [shuttle](https://scctd.org/services/midamerica-airport-shuttle/) between the Shiloh-Scott MetroLink station and the airport terminal.

## Support offices

- **Military & Family Readiness Center:** 618-256-8668 ([scottmfrc.com](https://scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Assistance))
- **Military OneSource:** 800-342-9647 ([militaryonesource.mil](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/))
- **All installation contacts:** [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS directory](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base)

## Utilities

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS points newcomers to [InMyArea.com](https://www.inmyarea.com/) to find utility providers by ZIP code.
