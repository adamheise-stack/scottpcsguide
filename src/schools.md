Schools are where Scott-area families most often get surprised, because the district map doesn't match the town map. Here's what the official sources say, plus the order we'd tackle it in. *Checked October 18, 2026.*

## Living on base

- **Mascoutah Community Unit School District 19.** On-base children attend District 19 schools: **Scott Elementary** (grades preK-5), then **Mascoutah Middle** (6-8) and **Mascoutah High** (9-12). Middle and high schoolers living in base housing are bussed. You must live on base to attend Scott Elementary. Sources: [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS Education](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/education/education) and the [Scott AFB School Liaison Office](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/School-Liaison-Office/).
- **A very military-connected district.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says Scott AFB children make up about 50% of District 19's students.

## Living off base

- **Your address picks the school.** Many local districts serve only elementary grades (preK-8) or only high school (9-12), and only a few cover preK-12. So one address can mean two districts, one for younger kids and one for high school.
- **Some nearby districts, by name.** The [St. Clair County Regional Office of Education](https://sccroe50.org/public-schools/) directory lists each public district in the county with a link and phone number, including Mascoutah #19, O'Fallon Grade #90 and O'Fallon High #203, Shiloh #85, Central #104, Belleville Grade #118 and Belleville High #201, Lebanon #9, and Freeburg Grade #70 and Freeburg High #77. A district's name doesn't tell you its boundaries, so always look up the exact address.
- **Private schools and homeschooling.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says there are many private schools in the area, and that Illinois homeschooling families register with the Regional Office of Education. The School Liaison Office can connect you with local homeschool groups.

## Comparing schools

The School Liaison Office points families to the [Illinois Report Card](https://www.illinoisreportcard.com/), the state's official source for public school data. It covers class sizes, teachers, school finances, and test scores and other academic results. Pull up a district's report card next to the home listings you're considering.

## Two Illinois allowances for military kids

The School Liaison Office describes two:

1. **Temporary quarters:** if you live in temporary quarters and have proof that you'll live in a particular district within 60 days, your child may attend that district without paying out-of-district tuition.
2. **Ordered on base:** if you live off base and must move on base due to military orders, your child may stay in the original district for the duration of those orders.

## Registering in Illinois

From [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/education/education) and the School Liaison Office:

- [ ] Certified birth certificate for each child
- [ ] Proof of residency (most districts want two, such as a lease or rent receipt and a utility bill)
- [ ] Up-to-date immunization record
- [ ] An Illinois physical. Illinois requires one for students entering early childhood programs, kindergarten, 1st, 5th and 9th grade, and for all students new to a district. An exam done within one year before entry counts. The School Liaison Office also lists Illinois dental and vision exam forms among the health forms.
- [ ] A Student Transfer Form if your child is coming from another Illinois school

**Kindergarten:** a child must be 5 before September 1. Districts offer part-time or full-day kindergarten, and MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says full-day kindergarten must be offered starting in the 2027-28 school year.

## Get help

The **Scott AFB School Liaison Office** (618-256-9595, by appointment per MilitaryINSTALLATIONS) can help with districts, transitions, special education, and the [Military Interstate Children's Compact](https://mic3.net/state/illinois/), which Illinois belongs to. The office also hosts monthly Parent Advocates for Students and Schools (PASS) workshops during the school year.
