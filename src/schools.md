## What the official sources say

*Checked October 18, 2026.*

- **Living on base:** children attend **Mascoutah Community Unit School District 19**. Scott Elementary (grades preK-5) serves on-base students; middle and high schoolers go to Mascoutah Middle and Mascoutah High. Source: [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS Education](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/education/education) and the [Scott AFB School Liaison Office](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/School-Liaison-Office/).
- **Living off base:** your school is set by your address. Many local districts are elementary-only (preK-8) or high-school-only (9-12), so one address can mean two districts.
- **Two Illinois allowances for military kids** described by the School Liaison Office: students in temporary quarters with proof they will live in a district within 60 days may attend that district without out-of-district tuition, and students who must move on base under orders may stay in their original district for the duration of those orders.
- **Get help:** the Scott AFB School Liaison Office (618-256-9595, by appointment per MilitaryINSTALLATIONS) can walk you through districts, transfers, and the [Military Interstate Children's Compact](https://mic3.net/state/illinois/).
