Picking a home sets your commute, your school district and your monthly budget all at once, so it's worth doing in the right order. Here's the order we'd follow as neighbors who have done it.

## Step 1: Talk to the Military Housing Referral Office

- **One office for both paths.** [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/housing) (accessed October 18, 2026) says prospective residents should begin their application with the Military Housing Referral Office, at 618-256-4900. It's also a good first call if you're leaning toward off base.
- **Lodging while you look.** The [Scott AFB Newcomers page](https://www.scott.af.mil/Newcomers/) says incoming PCS personnel can stay up to 30 days in Temporary Lodging Facilities and house hunters 10 days, if space is available. Call 618-256-2045 to request a reservation as soon as you know your arrival date.

## On base: Scott Family Housing

- **Privatized and managed by Hunt Companies.** The Scott Family Housing leasing office is at 618-418-4911 (MilitaryINSTALLATIONS).
- **Community extras.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says residents have two large community centers with one indoor swimming pool, meeting rooms and recreation areas for their exclusive use, along with parks, playgrounds and walking trails.
- **Schools.** Children who live on base attend Mascoutah Community Unit School District 19. See [Schools](/schools/).

## Off base: the surrounding towns

The [Scott AFB Military & Family Readiness Center](https://scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Assistance) names **Shiloh, O'Fallon, Belleville, Mascoutah, Fairview Heights and the greater St. Louis area** as the surrounding communities. A few honest tips for comparing them:

- **School districts don't follow town lines.** Many Scott-area districts are elementary-only (preK-8) or high-school-only (9-12), so one address can mean two districts. The School Liaison Office suggests looking up a property's district through the [St. Clair County Regional Office of Education](https://sccroe50.org/public-schools/).
- **Missouri is an option, and your BAH doesn't change.** The Air Force benefits site says you generally receive BAH for where you're assigned, not where you live. Factor in the longer commute.
- **Think about transit.** MetroLink's Red Line ends at the Shiloh-Scott station, and the SCCTD Flyer serves a Scott AFB zone. See [Local Services](/local-services/).

## Budgeting

Compare listings with your housing allowance. Our [2026 Scott AFB BAH guide](/guides/scott-afb-bah-rates-2026/) has the full rate table, plus the average local rents that Military OneSource publishes for the Scott area (for example, $1,542 for a three-bedroom house). BAH is calculated from rent *plus* utilities, so ask every landlord which utilities are included.

## Before you sign a lease

- [ ] Confirm the school district (or districts) for the exact address.
- [ ] Ask which utilities are included, and look up providers by ZIP code. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS points newcomers to InMyArea.com.
- [ ] Get your move-in date to line up with your household goods delivery.
- [ ] Have questions about a lease or your rights under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act? The Scott AFB legal office (618-256-5017) offers confidential consultations on topics including the SCRA ([Scott AFB Home Life](https://www.scott.af.mil/We-Care/Home-Life/)).
- [ ] Waiting on your furniture? The M&FRC's free Loan Locker lends folding tables, cookware, dishes and small appliances to newly arrived families (no linens). Call 618-256-8668.
