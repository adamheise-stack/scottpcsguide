## The short version

- **On base:** privatized family housing at Scott AFB ("Scott Family Housing") is managed by Hunt Companies, according to [Military OneSource MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/scott-air-force-base/housing/housing) (accessed October 18, 2026).
- **Start with the Military Housing Referral Office.** MilitaryINSTALLATIONS says prospective residents should begin their application there; it lists the office at 618-256-4900.
- **Off base:** the Scott AFB Military & Family Readiness Center names [Shiloh, O'Fallon, Belleville, Mascoutah, Fairview Heights and the greater St. Louis area](https://scottmfrc.com/Relocation-Assistance) as the surrounding communities.
- **Schools follow your address.** Before you sign a lease, check which school district serves it. See our [Schools](/schools/) page.
